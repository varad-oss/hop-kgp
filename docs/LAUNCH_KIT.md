# Hop launch kit

Link for everything: **https://hop-kgp.vercel.app**. The QR code on the home page points to the same link.

## 1. Hall WhatsApp group (main message)

> **Going home for winter break? Don’t book that CCU / Howrah cab alone.** 🚕
>
> I built **Hop** for exactly this, for KGP students only:
> 1. Post your station + day (Howrah, CCU airport, Santragachi or KGP station)
> 2. You land in one room with everyone from KGP going the same way that day
> 3. Tap “I’m in” on a plan, lock it, and the fare splits itself (₹2,800 sedan for 4 = ₹700 each)
>
> Your number stays hidden until the trip is locked: no more posting it in groups of 400.
>
> Continue with your @kgpian email ID, takes ~30 sec:
> 👉 https://hop-kgp.vercel.app
>
> Leaving 24–30 Nov? Post now so others find you. Coming back in Jan? Post that too.
